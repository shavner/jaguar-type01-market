How it works

  • Each student gets 100 chips over the answers.
  • All the chips form one pool. When the answer is known, the whole pool goes to the students that backed it, in proportion to their chips on it. If nobody backed it, nobody wins.
  • The answer is the first full-year 2027 figure SMMT publishes for Jaguar (all models) in its UK new car registrations by brand. Later revisions don't count.
  • This page fills in the result by itself in January 2028. The bets are frozen in the public history of this page, so you can check that nothing changed after class.
